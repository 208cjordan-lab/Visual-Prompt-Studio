Visual Prompt Studio 1.1- The Sci-Fi Film Poster Challenge

Create a poster of a science fiction blockbuster that features the landscape of an abandoned overgrown science testing facility with testing rooms with platforms and it displays robots working inside of the building making cubes being watched by an overseeing robot . The assembly lines should be enlarged, creating irregular and loose large vertical lines that, at the top are transformed into organic lines showing the deformation of society at the top. The Overseeing robot should be the largest shape, elongated and distorted into a more organic shape. The focus of the composition is on the robots that are working. This should contrast with the small shapes of cubes the robots are building. We can look at them through the windows. The texture of the city feels very metallic and rough strucures as a way to communicate that the robots dominate the scene. The mass of the robots should feel heavy. There should be a contrast of the laborers in vibrant, luminous colors and muting the person in charge into dull, flat tones, you shift the visual weight away from the figurehead.

Vocabulary breakdown:

Lines-
Basic element of any artwork and lines make up everything in the real world and in the art world. It all starts out as lines and shapes, and then becomes something beautiful entirely at the end. A line connects one point to the other end, connects planes, defines and outlines the shape, conveys movement and energy.
Shape- A two dimensional area, defined by lines or suggested by changes In value or color. Can be classified into two types- organic and geometric.
Texture- Texture refers to the way an object feels to the touch or looks as it may feel if it were touched. Plays a vital role in the storytelling of an object, painting, picture.
Volume- The amount of space an object takes up, defined by height, width, and depth.
Contrast- When artists use two different types of different states of an element such as using geometric shapes or irregular lines.
